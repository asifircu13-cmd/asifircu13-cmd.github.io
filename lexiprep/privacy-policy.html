LexiPrep Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Short version: LexiPrep has no accounts and no server of its own. Your study progress stays on your phone. The app is free and shows ads through AppLovin MAX, and AppLovin and its ad partners collect some device data to show and measure those ads.

Who we are

LexiPrep ("the app", "we") is a vocabulary app for GRE, GMAT, IELTS and SAT learners, published on Google Play under the package name com.lexiprep.vocab. Developer: Asif Mohammed Fahad. Contact: asifircu13@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Study progress: your goal, exam date, words learned, quiz results, streaks and settings. These are saved only in the app's storage on your device. We never receive them.
  • Backup codes: if you choose "Back up progress", the app makes a text code and opens your phone's share menu. Where you send it is your choice; we do not receive it.
  • Speech: word pronunciation uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine.
  • Reminders and widget: the daily reminder and home-screen widget run on your device only.

Uninstalling the app, or "Reset all progress" in Settings, deletes this data.

Data collected by ads (AppLovin MAX)

LexiPrep shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads through AppLovin MAX, which may pass ad requests to other ad networks it works with. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AppLovin and those networks may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (you can reset it or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language) and how you interact with ads
  • Crash and performance diagnostics of the ads software

This data is handled by AppLovin under its own policy: AppLovin Privacy Policy, and by any partner network under its own policy. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: the first time ads are possible, the app asks whether ads may be personalised using your advertising ID. No ads load until you choose. "No" still shows ads, just not personalised. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • Personalised ads: you can turn off ad personalisation in Android Settings or at adssettings.google.com.
  • Notifications: reminders are off until you turn them on, and Android asks your permission first.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share any data ourselves. When you tap a share button (WhatsApp, Facebook, Messenger, X, Telegram, LinkedIn or email), the app passes your chosen text to that app or website, which handles it under its own policy.

Children

LexiPrep is made for teenagers and adults preparing for exams and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: to load ads
  • Notifications: for the optional daily reminder
  • Run at startup: to restore the reminder after the phone restarts
  • Advertising ID: used by AppLovin and its ad partners as described above

Word content

Dictionary definitions come from Princeton WordNet 3.1 (© Princeton University), used under its license. Meanings, mnemonics, stories and Bangla translations were written for LexiPrep.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
