Tinkopolis – Privacy Policy

App: Tinkopolis (package com.tinkopolis.kids) · Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Developer: Asif Mohammed Fahad

Tinkopolis is a learning app for young children (around ages 2–6), made to be used with a parent or caregiver. We built it so that it does not collect personal information from children or adults.

What we collect

Nothing. Tinkopolis does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. The app has:

  • no accounts or sign-in,
  • no advertising and no ad SDKs,
  • no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs,
  • no chat, camera, microphone, location or contacts access.

The one-time "full version" purchase

The app is free to download. Grown-ups can optionally buy a one-time "full version" that unlocks every section. The purchase screen is behind a parent check. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store card or payment details. To know whether the full version has been bought, the app asks Google Play for the purchase status of the signed-in Google account and remembers the result on the device. We do not receive your name, email or payment information. Google Play's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

Information stored only on your device

To work, the app saves a few settings on the device itself: the daily screen-time limit and minutes used today, the voice language choice, skill medals, the parent checklist, the days the app was used, and — only if a grown-up enters them on the welcome screen or in the Grown-ups area — the child's first name and age, the grown-up's name and title, and a photo of the child. These are used only to personalise the app on this device (greetings, the home screen, the "School Ready" practice answers). A chosen photo is opened through Android's own photo picker, which gives the app only that one photo; the app keeps a small copy and does not upload it. This information never leaves the device, is not sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

The same applies to the optional emergency help button and the activity report. Only if a parent turns it on, the app saves up to three emergency contact names and phone numbers and a help message on the device. When the child holds the 🆘 button for 3 seconds, the app opens the phone's own SMS app with that message, or the phone's dialer with a number. Nothing is sent or called until a person presses Send or Call there. The app has no SMS, call or contacts permission, and it does not use or request location. The activity report (time spent in each section and recently heard words, kept for 30 days) is built on the device. It is sent only if a parent presses "Share report" and picks an app such as WhatsApp or email.

Shows

The "Shows" section plays the app's own animated cartoons, made inside the app from its own pictures and words. It does not stream videos, does not connect to YouTube or any other video service, and works offline like the rest of the app.

Speech

English words are spoken using the device's own text-to-speech engine, on the device. Bangla speech is played from audio recordings included inside the app. No voice or text is sent anywhere by the app.

Parental controls

The Grown-ups settings, the purchase screen and leaving the app are protected by a simple sum that young children cannot solve. Parents can set a daily screen-time limit, turn speech on or off, set up the emergency help button and view the activity report.

Children's privacy (COPPA, GDPR)

Because the app collects no personal information, no personal information of children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country) is collected, used or disclosed. If you believe otherwise, contact us and we will help.

Third-party content credits

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under their stated licences (credits are listed in the app under Grown-ups). Country facts come from Wikidata and Wikipedia.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
