About me

Tech enthusiast who loves turning ideas into apps.

I am a tech enthusiast who enjoys learning how things work and then building them myself. I hold a Master of Social Sciences (M.S.S.) in International Relations from the University of Chittagong, and my curiosity about technology led me to Android development.

I make apps to create the kind of simple, calm, useful tools I would want for my own family: apps that help children learn and help students prepare for exams, without clutter. Every app I publish teaches me something new.
